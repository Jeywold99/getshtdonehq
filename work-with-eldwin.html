case_01 · n8n + GoHighLevel + Monday.com client: confidential · one CRM account, three businesses

Vendor sourcing on autopilot

A contracting business needed to find subcontractors, collect their compliance documents, request quotes and track every bid to an award, with a team of one doing it by hand. I connected Monday.com, n8n, GoHighLevel, Gmail and Slack into one system where the team updates a status once and everything else follows.

// the problem

Vendor outreach was manual, quotes arrived in scattered inboxes, and nobody had one view of which vendors were vetted or which bids were still open. Outreach topped out at about 2 messages a day.

// what I built

  • Vendor bench CRM with prequal docs, trade and region
  • Two pipelines that move on their own from Monday updates
  • Vendor forms for prequal and quotes
  • A send gate that holds any email leaking sensitive bid details
  • Deadline alerts 7, 3 and 1 days out

// how it holds up

  • One system of record, synced one way, so data never forks
  • Safe to re-run: no duplicate bids, cards or vendors
  • Full SOP and test guide handed to the team

case_02 · n8n + GoHighLevel client: confidential · property company

From spreadsheet to sales pipeline

The same client's property business had hundreds of cold owner leads in spreadsheets and no way to work them in the CRM. GoHighLevel's built-in importer can't create deals or fill deal-level fields, so I built the pipeline in GHL and an n8n import that does what the importer can't.

// in GoHighLevel

  • 8-stage owner pipeline, cold lead to active management
  • Custom field dictionary: tier, close score, pitch angle, market
  • Missed-call text-back (live)
  • Landing-page intake workflow (built)

// re-engagement

  • 21-day retargeting campaign across 5 workflows (built)
  • Stall detectors for discovery calls and sent proposals
  • New inquiries automatically pulled out of retargeting

// the import

  • 335 cold contacts imported and routed through n8n
  • Duplicate check before every create
  • Every row ends labeled done or failed, never silent
