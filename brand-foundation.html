01 — Who We Are

The Mission

We are a no-bullshit coaching academy for virtual assistants and business owners. We don't sell theory — we get people moving.

We exist to turn overthinkers into executors — coaching marketers and business owners to stop consuming, build a crew that pushes each other forward, and actually get sh*t done.

01

Every student has untapped talent. Our job is to pull it out of them and point it at real, paying work — not give them another certificate to collect dust.

Nobody gets there alone. We build a community that motivates each other, answers the hard client questions, and refuses to let anyone quit halfway.

03

Coach to Execution

We're coaches, not lecturers. Every lesson ends in action. The win isn't finishing the module — it's the result the student creates after it.

02 — The Mark

Logo Usage

The stacked GET SH*T DONE wordmark is the heart of the brand. The censored asterisk is intentional — it's bold, a little cheeky, and 100% us. Treat it with respect: give it room, keep it orange, never redraw it.

Clear Space

Keep a margin of at least X on every side, where X equals the height of the asterisk in the mark. Never let other elements, text, or edges crowd it.

160px

Minimum Width — Digital

Below this the asterisk and counters start to clog. For print, never reproduce the mark smaller than 32mm wide.

Shorthand

When space is tight (favicons, avatars, stamps), use the GSD monogram in Bebas Neue — never crop the full wordmark.

Approved Backgrounds

The orange mark needs contrast. Place it on white, cream, or our dark neutrals. Never set the orange logo on an orange field.

✓ White
✓ Cream
✓ Midnight
✗ Orange

✓ Do

  • → Keep the wordmark in brand orange (#FF5C00) on light or dark grounds.
  • → Give it generous clear space and let it breathe.
  • → Scale it proportionally — width and height together.
  • → Keep the censored asterisk exactly as drawn.

✗ Don't

  • → Recolor, add gradients, glows, or drop shadows to the mark.
  • → Stretch, skew, rotate, or condense it.
  • → Place it on busy photos or low-contrast backgrounds.
  • → Spell out "Shit" in the logo — the asterisk stays.

04 — Type

Typography

Two typefaces, two jobs. Bebas Neue shouts — big, condensed, uppercase display. Outfit speaks — clean, modern, and readable for everything else. Don't add a third.

Display
Bebas Neue
ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ 1234567890 *

Headlines, hero statements, numbers, ghost type, the logo's voice. Always UPPERCASE. One weight only.

UI & Body
Outfit
ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz 1234567890

Body copy, navigation, buttons, labels, forms. Weights 400–800. Sentence case. Workhorse of the system.

05 — Voice & Tone

How We Talk

We're the coach in your corner who calls out the excuses and means it. Direct, motivating, a little irreverent — never mean, never fluffy.

We don't motivate with hype.
We motivate by getting you moving.

We Are

  • → Direct — we say the thing nobody else will.
  • → Motivating — we believe in the student before they do.
  • → Action-first — every sentence points to a next move.
  • → Real — peers in a crew, not gurus on a stage.

We Are Not

  • → Corporate, jargon-heavy, or "synergy" types.
  • → Hype-merchants promising overnight riches.
  • → Condescending or shamey when someone's stuck.
  • → Vague — we never leave people guessing what's next.
